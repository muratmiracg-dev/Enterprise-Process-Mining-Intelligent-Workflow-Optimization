I built an Enterprise Process Mining & Intelligent Workflow Optimization
portfolio project around a Purchase-to-Pay process.

The platform analyzes a deterministic synthetic benchmark of 12,000 cases and
166,551 events. It combines PM4Py process discovery, transparent conformance
checking, bottleneck and rework analysis, an explainable temporal SLA-risk
model, and replicated capacity simulation.

Key benchmark findings:
• 62.08% SLA adherence
• 21.98% of cases with rework
• 0.822 temporal-holdout ROC AUC
• Recommended combined scenario: 19.42% modeled cycle reduction and +13.63 pp SLA

I also delivered Power BI/PBIP, PostgreSQL, FastAPI, Docker, BPMN, Prometheus,
Grafana, CI/security workflows, 58 tests with 96.97% coverage, and bilingual
executive outputs.

All data and financial estimates are synthetic; simulation results are decision
hypotheses, not guaranteed savings.

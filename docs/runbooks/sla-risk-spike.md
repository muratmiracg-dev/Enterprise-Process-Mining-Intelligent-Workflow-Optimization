# Runbook: SLA risk spike

1. Confirm the signal is not caused by missing or delayed events.
2. Segment by business unit, priority, vendor tier, activity, and resource role.
3. Inspect high-risk cases and dominant bottleneck transitions.
4. Protect payment and approval controls; do not bypass them to improve the KPI.
5. Apply temporary capacity or queue triage with an accountable owner.
6. Measure SLA and rework after intervention; record unintended effects.
